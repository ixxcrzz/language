06 — GROUP SUMMARY

Our group perspective.

Our group mainly uses Standard English and Guyanese Creole, with some use of Spanish depending on the setting and who we are speaking with. Standard English is mostly used at university, work, church, and in formal writing, while Creole is more common with family, friends, and in the community because it feels more relaxed and natural. Spanish may sometimes cause confusion or humorous reactions from people who do not understand it.

Some challenges we may face include switching between Creole and formal English, pronunciation, grammar, and using unfamiliar words or terms. We may also have to be careful not to mix casual language with formal academic writing. Overall, we hope this course helps us understand how and why people change the way they speak in different situations, while also helping us improve our communication skills.
